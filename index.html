      Are you an official Syngenta distributor?

      Yes. Prime Agro is the official distributor of Syngenta vegetable seeds, including watermelon, cucumber, tomato, cabbage and carrot varieties.

      What is Fytomax and which pests does it control?

      Fytomax is a neem-based bio-pesticide. It targets Tuta absoluta, thrips, whiteflies, mites and aphids, stops pest feeding, and helps prevent egg-laying. It has a 1-day pre-harvest interval.

      How much does Fytomax cost?

      Fytomax is $15 for a 500 ml bottle. Message us for current stock and bulk pricing.

      Can you help me choose the right product?
